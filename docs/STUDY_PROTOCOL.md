# Proposed student study — not conducted

Recruit consenting adult students later, with an actual campus and public sources selected first. Record consent separately from task records. Do not collect credentials, raw class titles or calendar files in study results; let participants stop/delete their data. No participants or results currently exist.

Use the equivalent tasks in `eval/study_tasks.json`. Participants find relevant public events, verify official source/time/price, and select an event that does not conflict with a supplied or privately entered class schedule. No real registration or payment.

Compare manual browsing (condition A) with CampusScout (condition B). Counterbalance order AB/BA and assign equivalent task variants across conditions so familiarity is not confused with speed. Pilot the tasks for comparable difficulty before measuring. Record timing from task start to final choice or explicit failure; cap at a predeclared time limit (proposed 10 minutes). Define success before recruitment: correct interest/date/budget match, verified official source, and conflict-free selection.

For every trial record: pseudonymous participant ID, consent confirmed, order, task, condition, completion seconds, success/failure, reason, schedule error, source verified, and optional free-text feedback without private details. Empty template is supplied; there are no simulated rows. Report recruitment, exclusions and failed tasks explicitly. Compare paired completion times only with a disclosed failure policy; report all failure rates and avoid presenting fast failed tasks as time savings. Do not generalize from a convenience sample without limitations.

Keep raw de-identified records under ignored `eval/private/`. Only publish aggregate results after consent and review. Choose sample size with available student access and desired uncertainty before recruitment, not after looking at results. Student feedback is user research, not evidence of a development team.
